Instructions for bulk update
===================================================================

Start from the sample file and only change the values, not the columns.

  * Every cell you leave blank is left as it is on the product. Fill in only what you want to
    change - that is the whole point of an update.
  * The file has 27 product columns (A to AA) followed by a block of 6 variant columns (AB to AG).
    So a file is 33 columns wide for one variant, 39 for two, 45 for three, and so on.
  * Do not add or remove columns. Every column is read by its position, so one extra or missing
    column shifts everything after it. The upload is refused if the count does not fit.
  * Comma, semicolon and tab separated files are all accepted.
  * Leave any optional column blank if it does not apply. Blank is always safe.
  * The whole file is checked before anything is saved. If one row is wrong, nothing is
    imported and the row number is reported.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings you never have to decode
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Columns U to AA used to be number codes. They are written in plain words now - the same words the
upload template uses. Leave one blank and that setting is left exactly as it is.

Availability is worked out for you: fill in a stock column and the product or variant is marked
available unless the figure is 0.

There is no seller_id column. An update never moves a product to a different seller - the product
already has an owner.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
Product columns
-------------------------------------------------------------------

A.  product_id - mandatory. The ID of the product you are changing, from
      Products > Manage Products.

B.  category_id - the Category ID from the Categories page. Blank leaves the category as it is.

C.  product_type - exactly simple_product or variable_product, or blank to leave it as it is.
      simple_product   - one price, no options. Fill one variant block.
      variable_product - has options such as size or colour. Fill one variant block per option
                         and give attribute_value_ids on each.

D.  name - the product name. Changing it also updates the product's web address.

E.  short_description - optional. One line shown next to the product.

F.  description - optional. The full description.

G.  image - the main image. Copy the path from the Media page,
      for example uploads/media/2021/07.jpg

H.  other_images - optional. More image paths, separated with commas.

I.  tags - optional. Search words, separated with commas.

J.  sku - optional. Your own code for the product.

K.  stock - optional. How many you have. Fill this when you keep one stock figure for the whole
      product. Leave it blank and use the variant stock column instead when each option has its
      own stock. Leave both blank if you do not track stock.

L.  tax - optional. The tax ID from Products > Tax.

M.  made_in - optional. For example India.

N.  warranty_period - optional. Free text, for example 6 months.

O.  guarantee_period - optional. Free text, for example 1 year.

P.  video_type - optional. Exactly youtube or vimeo, or blank.

Q.  video - optional. The video link.

R.  minimum_order_quantity - optional. The fewest a customer can buy. Blank means 1.

S.  quantity_step_size - optional. Customers buy in multiples of this. Blank means 1.

T.  total_allowed_quantity - optional. The most one customer may buy. Blank means no limit.

U.  cod_allowed - Yes or No. Whether customers can pay cash on delivery.

V.  prices_include_tax - Yes or No. Whether the prices you typed already include tax.

W.  returnable - Yes or No.

X.  cancellable_until - No, Until received, Until processed, or Until shipped.

Y.  food_type - Not a food product, Vegetarian, or Non-vegetarian.

Z.  delivery_area - Everywhere, Only these pincodes, Except these pincodes, or
      Not deliverable yet.

AA. pincodes - only needed when delivery_area is "Only these pincodes" or "Except these
      pincodes". Separate them with commas or spaces.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
Variant columns - repeat these 6 for every extra variant
-------------------------------------------------------------------

AB. variant_id - the ID of the variant you are changing, from the product edit screen. This is
      what marks a variant block as filled in, so leave the whole block blank to change nothing
      about the variants. The variant must belong to the product in column A.

AC. attribute_value_ids - the IDs from Products > Attribute Values, for example 3,4.
      Blank leaves them as they are.

AD. price - numbers only, no currency symbol. Blank leaves the price as it is.

AE. special_price - the discounted price. It must be lower than the price.

AF. sku_variant - your own code for this variant.

AG. stock_variant - stock for this variant.
